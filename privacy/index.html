Privacy Policy for English Picture Dictionary for Kids

Last Updated: November 15, 2026

English Picture Dictionary for Kids is an educational mobile application designed for children aged 3–9 to learn English words. We are committed to protecting the privacy and safety of children and their families.

1. Summary of Our Privacy Practices

No Personal Data Collected: We do not collect, store, or share any personal information from children or adults.

No Account Registration: The app does not require creating an account, logging in, or providing a name, email address, or age.

No Behavioral Advertising: The app contains no third-party behavioral ads or tracking SDKs.

Offline & Local Audio: All word pronunciations are pre-recorded and bundled inside the app.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect any personally identifiable information (PII) or sensitive device data, including:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or physical addresses
  • Precise or approximate location data (GPS)
  • Photos, videos, contacts, or calendar data
  • Persistent device identifiers (such as IDFA) for tracking or profiling

3. Use of AI-Generated Audio

English Picture Dictionary for Kids uses pre-recorded audio files created with an artificial intelligence (AI) text-to-speech tool to provide English word pronunciations.

  • No Data Collection or Real-Time Processing: All voice audio is generated prior to app release and stored locally/statically within the app. When a user taps a word to hear its pronunciation, no data is transmitted to any AI provider or third-party server.
  • No Voice Recording: English Picture Dictionary for Kids does not access the device microphone, record user voices, or collect any biometric or voice data from children or adults.

4. Third-Party Services & SDKs

English Picture Dictionary for Kids does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising networks, social media plugins, or external data-collection SDKs.

5. Parental Rights Under GDPR & COPPA

Because we do not collect or store personal data from children or parents, there is no personal data on our servers to access, modify, or delete. However, under the GDPR (including Article 8 regarding children’s data) and COPPA, parents and legal guardians have the right to:

  • Review our privacy practices and ask questions about how the app operates.
  • Refuse any future collection of their child's personal information.
  • Contact us at any time to verify that no data associated with their child is held by us.

6. Data Security

Because all learning content and audio files operate locally on the user's device and no personal data is transmitted over the internet, the risk of unauthorized access or data breach is minimized.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect app updates or changes in legal requirements. Any updates will be posted at this URL with a revised "Last Updated" date. If we ever make material changes that affect how children's data is handled, we will notify parents and obtain verifiable parental consent prior to implementing those changes.
